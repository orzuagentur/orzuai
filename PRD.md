# OrzuAI - User Flow v1

## Landing Page

When a user visits the platform, they should see a modern, minimalistic landing page.

Elements:

* OrzuAI logo
* Short tagline
* Modern dark background
* Single centered button

Button:

START

After clicking the button, the authentication modal should appear.

---

## Authentication

Available sign-in methods:

1. Continue with Google
2. Email & Password

---

## Google Authentication

The user clicks:

Continue with Google

After successful authentication:

* Redirect directly to Dashboard
* No email verification required

---

## Email Registration

The user enters:

* Email
* Password

Buttons:

* Create Account
* Already Have an Account

After registration:

* A verification email is sent
* The user sees a confirmation screen

Message:

Check your email to verify your account.

After successful verification:

* Access to Dashboard is granted

---

## Email Login

The user enters:

* Email
* Password

After successful login:

* Redirect to Dashboard

---

# Dashboard

## Sidebar Navigation

The left sidebar contains:

1. Overview
2. Chats
3. Contacts
4. Knowledge Base
5. Integrations
6. AI Assistant
7. Analytics
8. Settings

Subscription must NOT appear in the sidebar menu.

---

## User Profile Section

Located at the bottom-left of the sidebar.

Displays:

* User Avatar
* Full Name
* Email Address
* Current Plan

Button:

Manage Subscription

---

## Overview

Dashboard overview cards:

* Total Messages
* Unique Contacts
* AI Responses
* Conversion Rate

Additional sections:

* Activity Chart
* Recent Conversations
* WhatsApp Status
* AI Status
* Quick Actions

---

## Chats

Displays all WhatsApp conversations.

Features:

* Read conversations
* Send manual replies
* Enable AI
* Disable AI

---

## Contacts

Displays all customer contacts.

Fields:

* Name
* Phone Number
* Last Contact
* Total Messages

---

## Knowledge Base

AI training and business information section.

Users can add:

* Services
* Pricing
* FAQ
* Business Hours
* Documents

The AI assistant uses this information when generating responses.

---

## Integrations

Available integrations:

* WhatsApp

Only WhatsApp is included in Version 1 (MVP).

---

## AI Assistant

AI behavior configuration.

Fields:

* Role
* Instructions
* Language
* Communication Style

---

## Analytics

Business statistics and performance insights.

Metrics:

* Messages
* AI Responses
* Contacts
* Activity

---

## Settings

Account and business settings.

Fields:

* Business Name
* Logo
* Email
* Password
* Delete Account
